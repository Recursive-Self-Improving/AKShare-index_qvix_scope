## Retrieval observations and interpretation

- The five CSI PDFs were successfully read directly from the CSI public host; each retrieved factsheet is dated 2026-10-30. The PDF text extraction interleaves table columns, but the actual 基日 and 发布日期 fields and values are present on page 1. Both SZSE announcements were successfully read directly as HTML, including their code/date tables and release language. These are observed document contents, not conclusions from an HTTP failure or from search snippets.
- Base date is an index calculation anchor, not an assertion that the index was publicly published or investable on that date. For historical rows dated strictly before the official release date, a pre-release/retrospective flag is warranted; on the release date itself the date-only evidence supports no such flag. This classification does not establish exactly when during the release day values became available, or prove contemporaneous tradability of any product.
- The user reports Sina sample history for `sh000680` beginning in 2020 and `sh000300` in 2002. Those sample observations were not independently re-retrieved for this report. If confirmed by the implementation's own evidence, they predate the verified releases (2025-01-20 and 2005-04-08 respectively) and must not be presented as contemporaneously published index values. The reported 2002 `sh000300` history even predates its official 2004-12-31 base date: do not conflate earliest vendor row with base date or launch date, and do not infer the vendor's reconstruction methodology from the dates alone.
- The [SZSE 2014 release article](https://www.szse.cn/aboutus/trends/news/t20140618_518484.html) reports 创业板50 performance from its 2010 base through May 2014 despite its 2014-06-18 release, a direct official example of pre-release performance being presented retrospectively. It does not establish historical tradability.
- These sources identify the six-digit indices and exchange contexts. The `sh`/`sz` symbol spelling follows the requested vendor convention; the official factsheets themselves do not verify Sina's prefix syntax. CSI's 发布日期 is used as the date-level public-release boundary; the research does not establish earlier limited disclosures or intraday availability.
