## Meaning, units, publication and external limits


Offline verification: patched `http.get_buffer` to return `io.BytesIO` of the retained body, cleared the pinned cached helper before and after invocation, and compared `index_option_50etf_qvix().to_csv(index=False)` byte-for-byte to the evidence CSV. Result: exact match, 2,849 rows, 2015-02-09–2026-11-13; duplicate closes 15.92 and 15.90 confirmed. No network used in replay.

[AKShare's pinned source](https://github.com/akfamily/akshare/blob/v1.20.53/akshare/index/index_option_qvix.py) names this `50ETF 期权波动率指数 QVIX` and links [provider's 50ETF page](http://1.optbbs.com/s/vix.shtml?50ETF). The [provider page](https://1.optbbs.com/s/vix.shtml) labels 50ETF separately, displays values with `%`, describes its own volatility index as using the SSE variance-swap algorithm and a closed-form algorithm, warns that it may contain errors, and says commercial use needs authorization. It is a provider-produced option-volatility measure, not ChinaScope news sentiment, not the underlying index close, and not seven separate volatility indices. Its label can conservatively say `50ETF QVIX 收盘（%）`, explaining percentage-point readings, with provenance and quality warnings.

The [SSE announcement](https://www.sse.com.cn/market/sseindex/diclosure/c/c_20161104_4198915.shtml) describes a **different official index**, 000188, as based on near/next-near 50ETF options and expected 30-day volatility. This supports background, not identity or exact implementation equivalence with the third-party QVIX. The provider page claims the SSE algorithm but does not establish exact provider horizon, annualization convention, option selection, historical reconstruction or corrections. **Provider-specific horizon and annualization remain unknown** without stronger source evidence; do not assert 30 days or annualized for this feed merely by analogy. Exact `close` calculation/cutoff, publication timestamp, revision schedule and availability for trading at close are unknown. Observation dates are not availability timestamps; no same-day trading, safe lag, predictive, causal or point-in-time claim. The 2015 first row is observed provider coverage, not proof of first publication or impossibility of earlier records. HTTP availability, correctness, licensing/redistribution, and possible future provider format changes are external risks.
