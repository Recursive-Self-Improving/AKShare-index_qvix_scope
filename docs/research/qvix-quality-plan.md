# 50ETF QVIX partial-quality contract — proposal for PLAN-REVIEW

Research only, 2026-11-15. This replaces the strict duplicate/order rejection *proposal* in [qvix-feasibility.md](qvix-feasibility.md), not its historical observations or evidence. No implementation or ledger decision is implied. D9 selects one 50ETF QVIX series for all seven panels; D10 requires evaluation of correction, explicit partial quality, and rejection.

## Clarification and choice

Three bounded same-provider avenues: (1) the [HTTPS exact file](https://1.optbbs.com/d/csv/d/k.csv) returned the same bytes as the original plaintext HTTP response with certificate verification successful, (2) the [provider page](https://1.optbbs.com/s/vix.shtml?50ETF) warns of possible errors and describes membership and use terms, and (3) its [chart script](https://1.optbbs.com/s/js/vix50.js) uses that same k.csv for its historical chart. Observations and hashes: [qvix-quality-evidence.txt](qvix-quality-evidence.txt). No authoritative correction or explanation was found. HTTPS matching supports endpoint-delivered byte identity at the second retrieval, not original HTTP transport authentication, historical correctness, or independent corroboration. The pinned wrapper still requests HTTP; silently claiming its capture used TLS would be false. If future code changes transport to HTTPS while invoking the pinned wrapper, record the original wrapper URL, actual URL and verified TLS separately and capture exactly what the wrapper consumes; this is a transport override requiring review, not evidence of a provider correction.

Compare: **provider correction**, if actually delivered and evidenced, is best for the corrected new snapshot, but neither exists nor justifies rewriting old snapshots. **Explicit partial quality** retains usable unambiguous observations without asserting correctness or selecting disputed data; it incurs visible warnings, gaps, and a nonzero refresh status. **Total rejection** prevents use of 2,847 unambiguous dates in this response and is warranted for unparseable or untrustworthy structure, not merely for representable row-quality defects. Choose partial quality, subject to independent PLAN-REVIEW. Authenticated delivery is not a quality certification. Existing AC9's wording “validated machine-readable rows” must be read as structural validation plus disclosed quality, not complete quality; reviewer must explicitly assess acceptance of partial output and nonzero status.

## Firm data contract for implementation

1. **Raw validation, before windowing:** invoke `akshare.index_option_50etf_qvix()` in a fresh bounded subprocess, capture every HTTP response body and metadata before decoding, including redirects, and retain the exact body consumed by the pinned wrapper. Reject transport errors, non-200, redirect policy violations, HTML/empty/broken CSV, absent or changed required schema, wrapper exceptions, and any missing, malformed or non-canonical calendar date after strict parsing or any missing/nonfinite required OHLC, across the entire response, including outside the display window. Validate raw first-five-column values independently of coercive wrapper conversion so malformed input cannot disappear into NaT/NaN; replay wrapper and compare its entire output, including order and values. Finite values are not proof of economic correctness; do not add unsubstantiated value bounds. Save failure evidence, never promote malformed input.
2. **Deterministic normalization:** preserve raw body and source-order wrapper rows in immutable artifacts, with each candidate identified by one-based source data-row ordinal and OHLC; no remapping. Parse date to ISO and group the entire valid response by date, sort dates ascending, and only then filter the requested window. Numeric equality uses the pinned decoded finite numeric values, with signed zero equal; each distinct O/H/L/C tuple is a candidate. Repeated equal tuples are exact duplicates for this schema even if textual spelling differs; retain all occurrences and ordinals in the sidecar. One distinct tuple produces the original values, whether repeated or not; more than one tuple produces a single `date,open,high,low,close,quality` row with all four numeric fields blank and `quality=conflict`, even if only open differs or closes match. One tuple has `quality=ok` or `quality=envelope_anomaly` if high < max(open, close, low) or low > min(open, close, high); preserve values without repair. Candidate anomalies within a conflict are recorded independently. All quality categories and counts are computed, never hardcoded by date. Source-only dates remain rows, marked in sidecar relative to the selected calendar, not treated as incorrect or omitted. Unsorted input is normalized and counted, not itself a value conflict.
3. **Artifacts:** new immutable snapshot contains the captured body/transport, original wrapper CSV in source order, sorted unique-date `observations.csv` with blank conflict OHLC and quality, and `quality.json` keyed by ISO date with every duplicate occurrence and its ordinal/tuple, conflict and envelope flags, source-only dates, comparator-missing dates, counts, source/selected ranges and calendar snapshot identity/hash. Save quality categories and counts for full response and selected window explicitly; no implicit conflation. Sidecar conflict candidates are auditable back to raw row ordinals. Metadata records hashes of each artifact, actual and requested URLs, status, retrieval time, pin versions, source/selection ranges, row/date/candidate counts, ordering and relative-calendar facts, and algorithm/schema version. Serialize CSV/JSON canonically and forbid NaN. The raw source and wrapper output, not a changed manifest or a sidecar alone, are the evidence.
4. **Snapshot and status:** separate acquisition outcome from data quality. A structurally valid response creates and atomically promotes a fresh immutable whole-response snapshot even if partial. Set attempt `success` only for structurally valid response without conflicts, envelope violations, source-only dates, or in-range comparator gaps; otherwise `partial`, with separate issue counts and reasons. Unordered rows and identical duplicates alone are normalization notices, not partial, but disclose counts. Partial snapshot is usable and fresh, never described as full-quality success; fetch exits nonzero if any required feed is partial or failed. A transport/schema/fatal-validation failure records failure and does not replace the last usable whole snapshot. Promote the new run manifest atomically even on individual failures, linking its failed attempt to the previous usable QVIX snapshot as stale, with original timestamp and quality preserved; if none exists, snapshot is null and chart unavailable. If manifest validation or atomic promotion fails, keep the prior latest pointer untouched and return nonzero. Do not merge by date across QVIX revisions or use an older value to resolve a newer conflict; a later corrected response is a new independently evidenced snapshot. A partial new snapshot supersedes an older clean one, rather than concealing new ambiguity.
5. **Graph and gaps:** every one of the seven genuine index panels plots the identical 50ETF QVIX close series on its independent right axis, labelled `50ETF QVIX 收盘（%）`. Plot every selected source date, including source-only dates; union with comparator dates within observed QVIX bounds and use explicit null close at each missing comparator date and each conflict. `connectgaps=false`; do not interpolate, invent calendar sessions or extend outside observed range. Distinguish a missing source date from a conflict in hover/quality annotations and visible summary; show 13 and one respectively for this evidence, plus four source-only and eight envelope anomalies. These are initial evidence counts, not constants. Source-only dates and abnormal OHLC remain disclosed even though close is plotted. All seven receive the same date/value arrays and nulls. Display selected period, actual source range, partial/fresh/stale/unavailable, latest attempt status and time, source snapshot time and issue counts prominently; no fake values when unavailable.
6. **Independent offline verification:** recompute from retained raw GBK bytes with isolated pinned wrapper/cache, compare full source-order wrapper CSV byte-for-byte, then independently parse required raw fields and derive date groups, candidate ordinals/values, normalized CSV, sidecar and all metadata counts/ranges/hashes. Compare each artifact exactly and verify the comparator snapshot identity/hash before recomputing its gaps and extras. Verify failure/stale semantics and active-manifest references, all seven embedded arrays/axes/nulls/status warnings and absence of external HTML resources. Never derive expected CSV or sidecar solely from each other. Hashes detect changes, not authenticity. Network disabled during replay; reject any mismatch before promotion.
